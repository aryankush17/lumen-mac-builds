1. SCOPE
This policy covers (a) the Lead Engine desktop software and (b) the online
activation/licensing service that checks your License Key. It explains the
limited data WE receive, what stays on YOUR computer, and your responsibilities
for the business contacts you collect.

2. THE SOFTWARE RUNS LOCALLY
Lead Engine runs entirely on your computer. The following NEVER leave your
machine and are NEVER sent to us:
  - the business leads you collect (the "runs" folder and CSV files);
  - the content of the emails you send;
  - your email/SMTP/IMAP passwords and your Google Places API key (these are
    stored only in config.json on your device and are ENCRYPTED at rest with a
    key tied to your machine);
  - your sending logs and suppression list.

3. WHAT WE RECEIVE (LICENSING ONLY)
To activate and protect your licence, the Software sends our activation service
only:
  - your License Key;
  - a one-way fingerprint ("machine ID") of your device;
  - the activation/verification timestamp and the app version;
  - your IP address, as an automatic part of making that web request.
We use this solely to issue, verify, bind-to-one-device, and revoke licences,
and to offer support. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. SERVICE PROVIDERS WE USE
  - Hosting of the activation endpoint: [HOSTING PROVIDER, e.g. Vercel].
  - Licence records: [RECORDS PROVIDER, e.g. Airtable].
These providers process the licensing data above on our behalf under their own

5. SERVICES YOU CONNECT (SEPARATE CONTROLLERS)
When you use the Software it talks directly, from your machine, to:
  - the Google Maps Platform / Places API (using your own key) - governed by
    Google's terms and privacy policy;
  - your email and IMAP providers (using your own accounts).
We do not receive data from these connections. Your use of them is governed by
those providers' own policies.

6. THE CONTACTS YOU COLLECT - YOU ARE THE CONTROLLER
When you collect and email business contacts, YOU - not us - are the data
controller for that personal data. You are responsible for:
  - having a lawful basis to process and contact them;
  - giving recipients the privacy information your law requires;
  - honouring opt-outs and any access/erasure requests they make;
  - keeping the data only as long as you need it and securing it.
The Software helps (encryption at rest, automatic opt-out suppression, a
suppression list), but the legal responsibility for these contacts is yours.

7. SECURITY
Credentials and your API key are encrypted at rest on your device. The activation
service is reached over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; keep your computer
and accounts protected and your License Key confidential.

8. RETENTION
Licensing data is kept for as long as your licence is active and for a
reasonable period afterwards for records, support, and anti-fraud purposes, then
deleted or anonymised. Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall
the Software.

directly. For business contacts YOU collected, requests from those contacts go
to you as their controller, not to us.
